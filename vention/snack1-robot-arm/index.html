    Controls
    • 1–5 or click a blue joint: select
    • ← → (or A D): rotate selected joint
    • ↑ ↓: previous / next joint
    • G claw: grab what's between the fingers, again to let go · Space demo · T trail · R reset
    • Shapes tab → Pick, then click where the robot should put it; Q E turn it on the way
    • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan
    • Everything is solid: the arm stops before it goes into a shape, belt, bin or the floor
    • Camera: I J K L move · U O orbit · + − zoom · F fit all · V top view · H home, or the pad at the bottom
